# Sample Review Cards

| Question | Answer | Deck |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What does spaced repetition optimize? | Review timing, so cards reappear close to the point of forgetting. | Learning |
| What should a good card ask? | One clear fact, decision, or concept at a time. | Learning |

Q:: What does the Good rating do?
A:: It increases the card interval using the current ease value.

Q:: What does Again do?
A:: It resets repetitions, lowers ease, and schedules the card for a short retry interval.
